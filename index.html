About

Learning becomes meaningful when it feels lived, shared, and personal.

I am a Japanese educator and facilitator whose work connects language learning, cultural practice, and community care.

My work has taken me from teaching international university students to supporting children through calligraphy, cooking, music, and seasonal events—always with the aim of making unfamiliar ideas feel approachable and human.
